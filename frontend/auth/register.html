Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026  |  Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and encrypted password (bcrypt-hashed)
  • Profile Data: Profile picture (stored via Firebase Storage) and display preferences
  • Google OAuth Data: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google
  • Conversion History: Records of OCR conversions including filenames and extracted text (for registered users)
  • Usage Data: IP addresses for rate limiting and security purposes
2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain your account and the OCR conversion service
  • Store your conversion history for convenient access
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Send password reset emails when requested (via SMTP/Gmail)
  • Enforce rate limits and prevent abuse
3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored in plain text
  • Authentication uses JWT tokens stored in HTTPOnly cookies
  • Database hosted on MongoDB Atlas with encrypted connections
  • Profile pictures stored on Firebase Storage with automatic old-image cleanup
  • Server secured with Helmet.js HTTP security headers
4. Image Processing

Images uploaded for OCR conversion are processed in real-time using Tesseract.js. Uploaded images are not stored permanently on our servers. For registered users, the extracted text and filename are saved to your conversion history for your convenience. You may delete any history entry at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth 2.0: For optional single sign-on authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Storage: For profile picture hosting (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • MongoDB Atlas: For database hosting
  • Vercel: For application hosting and deployment
6. Cookies & Session Data

We use HTTPOnly cookies to store authentication tokens (JWT). These cookies are essential for maintaining your login session and cannot be accessed by client-side scripts. We also use sessionStorage for temporary UI state management, which is cleared when you close your browser.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may:

  • Delete individual conversion history entries at any time
  • Remove your profile picture from account settings
  • Request account deletion by contacting us
8. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
